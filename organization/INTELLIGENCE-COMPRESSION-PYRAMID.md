# Intelligence Compression Pyramid

Specialist: raw teknik evidence + local finding.  
Lead: duplicate/cluster + subsystem conclusion.  
Head: systemic risk + options + technical recommendation.  
Council: cross-functional trade-offs + dissent.  
Founder: yalnız kritik decision package: options, cost, time, risk, recommendation, dissent, evidence links.

Drill-down her zaman mümkün, fakat default ekran raw teknik çöplük değildir.
