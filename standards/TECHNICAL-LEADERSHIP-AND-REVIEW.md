# Technical Leadership & Review Standard

Information path:
`Specialist evidence -> Technical Lead findings -> Department Head systemic synthesis -> Cross-functional Council trade-off -> Founder critical decision`

Raw logs/code are drill-down artifacts; Founder default view is decision-oriented.

Consensus is not truth. Evidence beats vote count. Dissent is preserved.
No specialist self-approves final work. Department head reviews technical completeness; independent review verifies milestone integrity.
